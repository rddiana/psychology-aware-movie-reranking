# Limitations and responsible interpretation

- Mood is not observed directly; all psychology-informed variables are behavioral proxies.
- Expected completion is a model estimate, not an online causal outcome.
- Offline ranking cannot replace an online A/B test.
- Exposure constraints depend on policy choices and tolerance values.
- Popularity may encode both bias and genuine shared preference.
- Impression propensities are unavailable, so the logged-interaction data are subject to selection bias.
- V07 exact minimal repair has not completed; the repository contains its executable proposal, not a claimed final improvement.
- The KION data and original GPT-2 weights are not redistributed.
